# Changelog

Each release's notes on the GitHub Releases page come from its section here. Newest first; headings are `## <version name>`, matching line 2 of `version.txt`.

## 2.0.9

- Settings now opens from a button at the right of the search bar.
- New Fix contacts in Organize: finds empty contacts, contacts with no name, numbers saved twice and numbers missing a country code, and fixes them.
- Import and export moved from Settings to Organize.

## 2.0.8

- Long-press a contact to select several at once, then favorite, share, delete or add them to a label together.
- Deleted contacts can be brought back with Undo for a few seconds.
- An A–Z strip on the side of the contacts list jumps straight to a letter.
- Long-press a number or email to make it the contact's default.
- Linked contacts can be unlinked from the contact's menu.
- New setting to show only contacts with phone numbers.
- Sorting by last name now also shows names as "Last, First".
- The editor adds phonetic names, relationships, internet call addresses and chat addresses.
- Text or email everyone in a label from the label's page.

## 2.0.7

- The app stays in portrait when the phone is turned.

## 2.0.6

- Editing a contact no longer opens the date picker by itself. It only opens when you add a new date.

## 2.0.5

- Swipe left and right to move between Contacts, Highlights and Organize, as in Dialtone. Tapping a tab slides over to it.

## 2.0.4

- The headers in Settings, on a label's page and in Merge duplicates are now solid, so lists scroll behind them instead of showing through.

## 2.0.3

- The search bar stays at the top of Contacts, Highlights and Organize, and lists scroll behind it, as in Dialtone.

## 2.0.2

- Each release is now opened on a test phone before it's published, so a version that won't open can't be released.

## 2.0.1

- Fixed the app closing as soon as it was opened in 2.0.0.

## 2.0.0

A new version of Contacts, rebuilt from scratch with the same look. It updates over the old app and keeps your contacts, settings, on-device accounts and birthday calendar.

**What's different**
- Merge duplicates now links the duplicates into one contact, the way Google Contacts does, instead of combining them and deleting the copies. Nothing is deleted.
- Contacts are shown the way Android combines them, so someone saved in two accounts is one contact everywhere, with the same avatar colour and shape as in Dialtone.
- Recently viewed contacts in Highlights and dismissed duplicate suggestions start fresh.

**Everything else is still here**
- Contacts list with Your info, Favorites and letter groups; search by name, number or email.
- Contact page with the blurred photo header, Call, Text, Video and Email, and a menu to share, set a ringtone, move to another account or delete.
- Editor for every field, with types, custom labels, pictures and labels.
- Highlights: favorites in Expressive shapes, birthday cards and Recents.
- Organize: labels, Merge duplicates and Settings (sort order, calls and texts in Recents, the Contact Birthdays calendar, import and export, and accounts).
- Picking, adding and opening contacts from other apps, opening .vcf files, and a QR code for Your info.
