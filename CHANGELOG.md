# Changelog

Each release's notes on the GitHub Releases page come from its section here. Newest first;
headings are `## <version name>`, matching line 2 of `version.txt`.

## 1.0

First release of this fork of the Contacts app from vayun-mathur/Modern-Apps, restyled after
Google Contacts.

**Look and feel**
- Material 3 Expressive theme with dynamic colour, Google Contacts' surface layering, and all
  text in Google Sans Flex
- No hamburger menus: bottom navigation, with secondary actions in ⋮ menus
- New app icon: the GrapheneOS Contacts glyph in white on Common Teal
- Contacts list without the fast-scroll bar

**Highlights tab**
- Favorites row with large photos
- Upcoming birthday cards with ages and quick Call / Message buttons
- Recents: recently viewed contacts, or (opt-in) calls and texts with saved contacts, with
  call/text icons and message previews

**Contact page**
- Large round photo over a blurred copy of it, with a ring separating the two; contacts
  without a photo get a gradient of their colour
- Contact info and Labels cards in Google Contacts' style; Share, Set ringtone, Move and
  Delete in the menu

**Your info**
- Uses Android's Me profile, with a QR code to share your contact card

**Birthday calendar**
- Calendar named "Contact Birthdays", with the age each person turns in the event title

**Other**
- SIM contact support removed
- Editor: more space between field groups, so "Add phone" no longer crowds the email field
